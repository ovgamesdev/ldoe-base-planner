Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when using the LDOE Base Planner web application (hereinafter "Service"), developed by ovgamesdev.

The Service is an unofficial fan tool created solely for informational and entertainment purposes for Last Day on Earth players.

1. Data Collected

We collect the minimum amount of data required to ensure the operation of the Service:

  • Identification and Authentication (Firebase Auth): When using anonymous login, a unique anonymous user identifier (UID) is created. When authenticating via Google, your email address, display name, and profile avatar are processed.
  • User Content (Firebase Realtime Database): Data of base maps, object placement schemes, and layouts created by you, their unique identifiers, and technical link to your UID.
  • Protection and Security Data (Google reCAPTCHA v3 / Firebase App Check): Technical information about requests, verification tokens, and interaction metrics to prevent spam, automated attacks, and protect API endpoints.
  • Web Analytics (Google Analytics): Anonymized information about Service usage, including pages visited, session duration, user interaction, and device technical characteristics to analyze and improve the Service.

2. Purposes of Data Processing

  • Providing user authentication and saving game layouts.
  • Synchronizing saved layouts and maps between your devices.
  • Protecting the Service from malicious use, abuse, and DDoS attacks.
  • Improving planner stability and performance.
  • Analyzing user experience and activity to enhance Service capabilities.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service uses Google Firebase infrastructure, Google security services, and Google Analytics. Data processing is governed by Google’s respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Security and Privacy

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Your data is stored for as long as necessary to operate the Service. You have the right at any time to request complete deletion of your saved maps and account data by sending a request to our contact email.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and localStorage for two purposes:

  • Necessary: Firebase Authentication session — required to log in and use the site, cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (Firebase Analytics) — set only after your consent in the banner.

You can change your decision at any time by resetting your consent (the banner will reappear).
